# Series Title: Dexter (2006-2013)
## About the Series
**Dexter** is an American crime drama television series set in *Miami, Florida*.  
It follows **Dexter Morgan**, a forensic blood spatter analyst who leads a double life —  
as a **vigilante serial killer** targeting murderers who escaped justice.

#Trivia: The Bay Harbor Butcher
> "The Bay Harbor Butcher is a Miami serial killer who secretly hunts other serial killers that escaped justice, dismembers them, and dumps their bodies at sea."

**Did you know?**  
Dexter himself is the **infamous Bay Harbor Butcher**, hiding in plain sight while working for the Miami Metro Police.
